Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

Our Request a Quote form asks for your name, company or property name, phone number, email address, property address, city, property type, services requested, preferred service timing, and any message you choose to include. We also ask for your consent to be contacted before the form can be submitted.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through this website is used only to respond to your request for a commercial quote or site walkthrough. Submitting the form opens an email addressed to us with your information pre-filled, which you then choose to send from your own email account. This website does not store your submitted information in a database.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.
