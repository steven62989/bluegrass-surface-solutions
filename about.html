About Bluegrass Surface Solutions

Commercial pressure washing and exterior maintenance for Central Kentucky properties.

Our Company

Bluegrass Surface Solutions was founded by Jon Hansel and Steven Livingston to provide commercial property managers, apartment communities, retail centers, restaurants, offices, medical buildings, and contractors with reliable exterior cleaning services across Richmond, Lexington, Berea, Georgetown, Nicholasville, Winchester, and the surrounding Central Kentucky area.

We focus exclusively on commercial properties, working with property managers and business owners to schedule cleaning around their operations. Our approach is built around clear service scopes, flexible scheduling, and recurring maintenance programs that keep properties looking their best.

Our Approach

  • Reliable, Scheduled Service
  • Clear Service Scopes
  • Flexible Scheduling Options
  • Recurring Maintenance Available
  • Before-and-After Documentation
  • Clean, Professional Property Presentation
